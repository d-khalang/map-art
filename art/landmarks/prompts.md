# Landmark sticker generation recipe

Stickers are isolated PNGs with a fully transparent background. The renderer adds a thin cream edge and a soft drop shadow, and prints them about 5–6.5 mm wide, so they only need a clear silhouette.

## Shared style prompt

> Create one isolated landmark sticker of [LANDMARK]. Soft anime-film watercolour (Ghibli-like): gentle washes, warm light, light brown ink linework, simplified but architecturally recognizable. Three-quarter frontal view, centered, generous transparent margin, no map, no frame, no badge, no white border, no text, no label, no letters. Fully transparent background outside the object, crisp readable silhouette, designed to remain legible at about 5–7 mm wide in print.

## Subject directions

- **Utrecht — Dom Tower:** tall freestanding Gothic bell tower, warm stone, pointed arches, strong vertical silhouette.
- **Utrecht — Rietveld Schröder House:** compact De Stijl house, white and grey planes, black lines, restrained red, blue, and yellow accents.
- **Utrecht — Railway Museum:** historic red-brick Maliebaan station, symmetrical façade, central clock and arched windows.
- **Domodossola — Piazza Mercato:** characteristic pastel arcaded façade, stone columns, slate roof and Alpine market-square character.
- **Domodossola — Palazzo Silva:** compact grey-stone Renaissance palace with regular windows and dignified civic façade.
- **Domodossola — Sacro Monte Calvario:** hilltop sanctuary complex with church and small chapels, Alpine setting kept within the sticker silhouette.
- **Zaltbommel — St Martin's Church:** large Gothic church with a massive unfinished flat-topped tower and red-brick nave; no spire.
- **Zaltbommel — Stadskasteel:** red-brick fortified townhouse with stepped gables, small conical corner turrets, and patterned shutters.
- **Zaltbommel — Waterpoort:** small modest Dutch red-brick gatehouse integrated into a town wall, one broad passage arch plus one adjacent narrower arch, compact stepped gable, shallow dark tiled roof, restrained blind brick arcades; no round towers, turrets, castle keep, battlements, or fantasy architecture.
